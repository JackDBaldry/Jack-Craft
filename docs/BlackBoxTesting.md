# Black Box Testing by a Peer

First test, no advice: 

Number of runs: 10

It was a cool concept and I like the use of graphics and backgrounds as well as the dynamics of how the player moves as the fox.

As a console player and no explanation of controls, I found that hard to pick up but that's more because I'm not used to it. 

I was unsure if what was good and what was bad at the start but I figured it out fairly quickly. 

I was surprised that your ankle to die and come back to life of you can get out of the negative numbers, that's a game mechanic I haven't seen before. 

I found it a bit difficult to get enough carrots to be able to stay alive very long as there were more bad things than good things. 

It was good game design to create borders for the player so that you can't just walk off the map. 

I found everyday that I could stay alive longer by not moving from the center and just trying to jump over the bears rather than moving around trying to dodge everything frantically.
